Grocery Sales privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026.

What the app does

Grocery Sales saves a list of grocery items you choose and shows their current prices at Australian supermarkets. Everything the app stores lives on your phone.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. We do not run a server and we never receive your list, your prices or anything else from your device.

Data stored on your phone

Your saved items, the prices the app has fetched for them, your chosen IGA store and your display settings are kept in a local database on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Nothing is backed up or synced by us.

Network requests

To show prices, the app requests product pages directly from the supermarkets' public websites (Woolworths, Coles, IGA, ALDI and Harris Farm) from your phone, the same way a web browser would. Those retailers see a normal web request from your device, including your IP address, and their own privacy policies apply to that. The app sends them only the product searches and product ids needed to fetch prices. It sends nothing to us.

Tapping a product opens the retailer's page in your browser.

Permissions

Internet access only, used solely for the requests described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising or purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released and the date above will change.
