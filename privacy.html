SpecialList privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026.

SpecialList is published on Google Play by SpecialList (legal name: Michael David Gow).

What the app does

SpecialList saves a list of grocery items you choose and shows their current prices at Australian supermarkets. Everything the app stores lives on your phone.

Data we collect

Nothing automatically. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting service and no advertising. We do not run a server and the app never sends us your list, your prices or anything else from your device by itself. The only data that reaches us is feedback you choose to send, described below. To work, the app sends product searches directly to the supermarkets and barcode numbers directly to Open Food Facts, as described below; those requests do not pass through us.

Feedback you choose to send

The menu has Send feedback or report a problem. It opens your own email app with a message to us that already contains: the app version, your Android version and phone model, any errors the app recorded, whether each supermarket answered at its last check, and every one of your lists: the searches, items and links in it, each item's prices and when they were checked, and what the last check asked each supermarket. You can read all of it, change it or delete it before sending, and nothing reaches us unless you press send. We use what you send only to answer you and fix the problem. We keep feedback emails until the problem is resolved, then delete them within 12 months. You can email us at any time to ask us to delete yours sooner.

Data stored on your phone

Your saved items, the prices the app has fetched for them, your chosen Coles and IGA stores and your display settings are kept in a local database on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it from the app. Android may include it in your phone's own backup to your Google account, which we cannot see. Back up lists and prices saves a copy as a file in a place you pick, such as your phone's storage or your own Google Drive; we never see that file either.

Network requests

To show prices, the app requests product pages directly from the supermarkets' public websites (Woolworths, Coles, IGA, ALDI and Harris Farm) from your phone, the same way a web browser would. Those retailers see a normal web request from your device, including your IP address, and their own privacy policies apply to that. The app sends them only the product searches and product ids needed to fetch prices, and the store you chose at that shop. It sends nothing to us.

Because Coles sometimes moves the address its prices come from, the app downloads, about once a day and whenever Coles stops answering, one small file from this website that holds the current address. That request carries nothing about you or your lists. GitHub, which hosts this website, sees a normal web request including your IP address. We do not receive it.

Tapping a product opens the retailer's page in your browser.

Permissions

Internet access is used for the requests described above. Weekly background price watches are off by default. If you enable them, the app checks your tracked products and confirmed same-pack matches using Wi-Fi or mobile data while the app is closed. Android controls when checks can run. Failed checks may be retried twice. You can turn the feature off in Settings > Price watches.

Notification permission is requested only when you enable weekly background checks. Notifications are generated on your device for price drops, price targets and failed checks; we do not run a push-notification server.

Barcode scanning

The Android barcode scanner is bundled inside SpecialList and requests camera permission when used. Barcode processing happens on your device; camera images are not uploaded or saved. No scanner app or module download is required. You can type a barcode instead. For an unknown food barcode, SpecialList sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts to identify the product, then searches supermarket listings by its name. Open Food Facts receives the request and your IP address; no camera images, loyalty card codes or aisle notes are sent. Catalogue details are suggestions and require you to confirm the exact supermarket pack. Product data attribution: Open Food Facts, Open Database License (ODbL). Confirmed barcode-to-product links, price targets, shopping quantities and categories are stored locally and included in exported backups. Barcode links do not automatically verify supermarket product identity.

Loyalty cards and aisle notes

Saved loyalty card names, barcode values and formats are stored locally and included in exported backups. Keep those files private. SpecialList does not log into your loyalty account, retrieve points or upload your card details. Barcode values are displayed only when you open a card. Saved branch names and aisle notes are also local and backed up; notes are your own observations, not live supermarket location data. Opening a supermarket link hands the product page to your browser.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly receive personal information from children.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising or purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released and the date above will change.
